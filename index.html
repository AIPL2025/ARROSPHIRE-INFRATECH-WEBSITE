Our Legacy & Leadership

Multi-Generational Craftsmanship Meets Corporate Execution

Carrying forward an ancestral legacy in sacred architecture, Director Nagender Singh Yadav has been actively building Gurudwaras and Mandirs since 1996—completing over 66 landmark religious structures across Delhi, Haryana, Rajasthan, Uttar Pradesh, Punjab, and Uttarakhand.

In 2007, he expanded into residential real estate and turnkey home development across Uttam Nagar, Mohan Garden, Dwarka Mor, and Dwarka (10 km radius), serving 100+ families with transparent collaboration, sale/purchase, and contract home construction. Today, ARROSPHIRE INFRATECH PRIVATE LIMITED unifies both divisions under one registered corporate entity.
